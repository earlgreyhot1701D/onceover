METHODOLOGY & ARCHITECTURAL BOUNDARIES

Onceover uses a strict two-stage separation of concerns. All diagnostic detection is 100% deterministic, running standard axe-core rules and pattern matchers inside an isolated sandbox. Artificial intelligence never performs the scan, inspects raw HTML, or evaluates legal compliance; its role is strictly bounded to translating confirmed technical findings into plain-language, practical remediation steps for small business owners.

OVERVIEW

Automated checks evaluate code syntax and known structural patterns, but cannot determine genuine customer access. Crucial areas that require manual human review include:

ALT TEXT QUALITY

Whether an image description is genuinely meaningful or merely present (e.g. filename "photo1.jpg" vs "Warm sourdough loaves cooling on a cedar rack").

KEYBOARD FLOW & TRAPPING

Whether a shopper can smoothly tab through checkout, modal dialogues, or menus without getting trapped or losing visible focus.

LOGICAL READING SEQUENCE

Whether screen readers announce page sections, specials, and pricing tables in an intuitive sequence that reflects visual grouping.

SENSORY-ONLY INSTRUCTIONS

Directions that rely solely on color, shape, or position (e.g., "click the round orange button on the left to place order").

CAPTION & TRANSCRIPT ACCURACY

Whether promotional video captions accurately convey speech, speaker turns, tone, and critical sound cues.

PLAIN LANGUAGE & COGNITIVE CLARITY

Whether policies, allergy disclaimers, return instructions, and form validation errors are straightforward to read and comprehend.
